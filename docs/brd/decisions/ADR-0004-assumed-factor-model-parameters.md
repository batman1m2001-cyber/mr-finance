# ADR-0004 — Assumed factor-model parameters, labelled “giả định”

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-15
- **Sources:** docs/NEWS_IMPACT_PLAN.md A1; BRD §9

## Context

BRD §9.2 estimates volatilities and correlations from 55 months of history (2/2022–8/2026) and gives the S/M/L sizes and realisation per factor; the covariance values themselves are not in the BRD. We have no Vietnamese history series (VN-Index, foreign flows, SJC spread, VN rates).

## Decision

- `data/factor_model.json` (version FM-DEMO-2026-10, generated by `scripts/make_factor_model.py`) takes the BRD's 15 factors, sizes and realisation as given and **assumes** volatilities, correlations, R² and stress multipliers, each labelled “giả định”, shown on the Mô hình page (SCR-CIO-07).
- The stressed matrix follows §9.1 exactly; its eigenvalue repair is recorded in the model (smallest eigenvalue before repair −0.17, largest correlation change 0.10).

## Consequences

- Every result says its parameters are assumed. Estimating them is **blocked** on data (Q-22); the estimation script is a documented boundary.
