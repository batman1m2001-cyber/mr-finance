## Intended behaviour
On the client and RM overviews: effective runs over the pre-review threshold (FEAT-08), goal-branch matches
(FEAT-10), the dashboard's drawdown breach (FEAT-21), notable month-on-month changes. Each item says why it is
shown and links to its evidence page. It would replace the overview's “Headline News” card and resolve Q-03.

## Acceptance (plan gate)
Each item type has a test with its evidence link; an empty state.
