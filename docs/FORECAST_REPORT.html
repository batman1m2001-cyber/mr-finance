Forecast Desk Findings

Could we make the agents' 3-day and 1-week calls clearly better than a guess? We tried seven ways over two months of data, then ran one test month.

Result: no. The test month failed the target.

No method picks the direction of a market better than a coin in a way that lasts from one month to the next. What does work is telling which markets are about to move a lot, without saying which way.

2 · How a forecast is scored

One example

Morning
01/08: four news events touch gold. Gold's typical 1-week move (σ) is 2.6%.
Agents
Their drafts average +0.2%, under the 0.25σ minimum, so it does not count as a call.
Odds model
Says up, small (+0.8%).
Reality
Gold rose 7.4% in the week, 2.9σ: a big move (≥ 1σ).
Score
Agents: missed · Odds model: direction right but it did not say big.

Direction = the share of calls that went the right way. Recall = the share of big moves that were called the right way. About 1 move in 6 is big.

4 · What is happening

Whoever wins one month loses the next

The agents' 63% in September looks good, but September markets mostly rose: always calling "up" would have scored 72% on the same moves.

5 · The test

September against the target

The setup was frozen from July and August before the test: the odds model, because the LLM desk added nothing to it.

6 · What next

Better data, not a better prompt

  • Keep: the big-move signal. It can warn "VN-Index is likely to move a lot this week" without saying which way.
  • Get: daily open, high, low and volume, plus money flows (foreign and proprietary trading). These are the inputs we lack.
  • Open: the long-run money check (weekly long/short since 2023) is written but has not been run yet.
  • After 28/10: rescore September once the last moves come due. The setup stays frozen.
