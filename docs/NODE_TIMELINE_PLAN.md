# Money on the edges and a node's timeline — plan

The owner's idea (2026-10-20): show the money on the edges between “Bạn” and the other nodes, signed (+ / −), and when
a node is clicked, show a timeline chart of that node's money by day, month or year — “with the timeline chart, you
have to create data for years”. Branch `feat/node-timeline`, off `main` after the 2026-10-20 UX fixes.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | An edge shows the **change** over the chosen period (“+12 triệu”), not the level; the node keeps its level (“1,92 tỷ”) | the level is already under the node's name; a change is what a sign means |
| D2 | One period switch **Ngày · Tháng · Năm** in the graph's controls, default **Tháng**; the inspector's chart follows it | one choice, seen in both places; a month is what Tổng quan already compares (“so với tháng trước”) |
| D3 | Day = since yesterday, month = since the same day last month, year = since the same day last year; all end at the demo date (2026-10-09) | the month figure is then exactly today's `value − prev_value`, the one Tổng quan shows |
| D4 | Labels only on the spokes that hold money: “Bạn” ↔ an asset class, a debt class, Thanh khoản dự phòng, Thu nhập / Chi tiêu | goals, risks, news and scenarios already carry their own figure, and it is not a level that changes by day |
| D5 | A debt's change is its own (“Dư nợ −20 triệu”) coloured by its effect on net worth (a smaller debt is green) | the number reads as the thing; the colour as good or bad |
| D6 | Income and spending: the period's sum (day = the latest day with rows, month = the flow's month, year = the last 12 months), “+” for in, “−” for out | a flow has no level, so its change is its amount |
| D7 | The label sits on the outer part of the spoke (≈ 62 % from the centre), a small pill, hidden when it would cover a disc or a label | the centre's end is crowded by every spoke |
| D8 | The timeline is in Thông tin, under the node's figures: a line of the level with the period's change as bars under it (a flow: bars only) | the inspector is where a node's numbers already are |
| D9 | Ranges: Ngày = the last 90 days, Tháng = every month since January 2021, Năm = 2021 … 2026 (2026 “đến 9/10”) | the owner asked for years; daily points over five years would be unreadable |
| D10 | History is **generated** by `scripts/make_history.py` into `data/history/<client>.json`, never hand-edited, and labelled “mô phỏng” wherever it shows | `docs/brd/MAINTAINING.md`: data/ is generated by scripts |

## T1 — the history (data)

`scripts/make_history.py` reads the fixtures (`data/clients/*.json`, `data/market.json`) and the stored market series
(`data/state.db`, `market.store.points`) and writes one file per person:

```
{"as_of": "2026-10-09", "note": "Dữ liệu mô phỏng …", "dates": [... month-ends 2020-12-31 … 2025-09-30, then every day 2025-10-08 … 2026-10-09 ...],
 "holdings": {"<holding key>": [value per date]},          # today's holdings, keyed as wealth.valuation keys them
 "closed":   {"<key>": {"class", "name", "values": [...]}}, # money that was elsewhere before it bought today's holdings
 "transactions": [... 2021-01 … 2025-09 rows, the fixtures' shape ...]}
```

Every series **ends at today's value and passes through the anchors the app already uses**: `prev_value` on
2026-09-09 and `ytd_value` on 2026-01-01 where the source gives one. A holding the sources give no month-ago value
for (cash, deposits, loans, bonds, insurance: `prev_value = value`) stays flat over the last month, as Tổng quan
already counts it.

| Holding | Path before today |
|---|---|
| Stock | quantity × the ticker's **real** daily close (HOSE, stored since 2020) × a correction that is constant before 2026 and log-interpolated through the anchors after it (the demo's prices are mock, so the real shape is kept and the demo's figures met) |
| Gold | quantity × the real SJC price, the same correction |
| Crypto | quantity × the real BTCUSD / ETHUSD × USDVND, the same correction |
| Fund | equity fund on the VN-Index's shape; bond and money funds a smooth NAV (their rate a year) with small noise; anchored |
| Bond | price from its average price on the day it was bought to today's, linear, flat over the last month |
| Real estate | from the purchase price in the purchase year to today's value, compounding monthly with small noise, through the month-ago anchor |
| Deposit | its balance from the day it was opened; 0 before |
| Cash account | today's balance with the salary-day saw tooth; flat over the last month |
| Insurance (unit-linked) | from 60 % of today's value in 2021, growing by its premiums |
| Loan | amortised backwards at its rate and monthly payment; flat over the last month |
| Card | a monthly cycle around today's balance (spent through the month, paid on the 25th) |
| Business, other declared | today's value in yearly steps (±5 %) |

Before a holding was bought it is 0, and the money it took (its value on that day) was in a **closed** deposit
(“Tiền gửi đã tất toán”) of the same person — so net worth does not jump on a purchase. The closed deposit also takes
the month's savings back out in reverse: going back in time, it grows by each purchase after that day and shrinks by
each month's income less spending. The script fails if a closed deposit or a cash account would go below zero.

A holding with no dates in the fixtures (`bought: None`) existed before 2021. A real-estate purchase year gives a
month from a seeded draw. Older transactions (2021-01 … 2025-09) are the person's own twelve months' rows, the same
day and category, with salary 6 % a year and spending 3,5 % a year lower going back, jittered; a Tết bonus (two
months' salary, late January) for salaried people. Seeded: run it again and nothing changes (with the same stored
market series; the files are committed, so the app never needs the store).

## T2 — the reads

`src/timeline/` (ops + graph, a feature folder like the others):

- `history.py`: load a person's file (cached by mtime); `series(keys)` sums the holdings and closed deposits of the
  people in scope; a holding missing from the file (declared in the app after the file was made) is flat at its
  value with its month-ago anchor.
- `change(holdings, node)`: `{day, month, year}` for a node: the family (“Bạn”, assets − debts), a class, a holding,
  the liquidity reserve (the same rule as `dashboard.liquidity`), Thu nhập / Chi tiêu (sums of rows).
- `POST /api/timeline {client_id, scope, node, unit: day|month|year}` → `{node, unit, kind: level|flow, points:
  [{t, label, value, change}], simulated: true, note}`; `auth.require_client`, as every family read.
- `overview.build` gets `changes` (one op before it): each money node gets `change: {day, month, year}`; flows
  get theirs from the transactions.

## T3 — the edges

`graph.js`: the spoke of a node with `change` gets a label pill at ≈ 62 % of its path (`getPointAtLength`), text
`vnd(x, true)` in `--up` / `--down` (inverted for debts), hidden when its box meets an obstacle (`obstaclesNow`). The
period switch in `.ws-ctl` (`seg`, `aria-pressed`), kept in `sessionStorage` (`mf.ws.unit`), redraws the labels only.
The edge's tooltip and the edge inspector say the period's change too.

## T4 — the timeline in Thông tin

`inspector.js`: for a money node, “Diễn biến · mô phỏng” with the same Ngày / Tháng / Năm switch, a chart drawn by a
new `charts.js` `timeline()` (SVG, no library: line + area for the level, signed bars for the change, hover with
date, value, change), the first and last values and the change over the range in words, and a note that the history
is simulated. Fetched on select (`/api/timeline`), cached per node and unit in the page.

## T5 — checks and docs

- Tests: the generator's invariants (every series ends at today's value, passes the anchors, no negative cash, is
  deterministic) on the committed files; `/api/timeline` shapes, scope (family ⊇ personal), access (an RM is refused a
  family that is not theirs); the overview's changes equal `value − prev_value` for the month; a UI test that a spoke
  shows a signed label, the switch changes it, and a click shows the chart.
- Screenshots desktop 1440×900 and phone 390×844, light and dark, of the graph with labels and the inspector chart.
- Docs: CHANGELOG, FEAT-40 (the graph) section, `api_reference` row, `regression_coverage` row,
  `check_brd_docs.py` at 0 errors.

## Result

All five phases built on `feat/node-timeline`.

**Changes from the plan, each decided while building:**
- **An estimate's spoke shows its own figure (owner, mid-build).** The owner asked: “Sụt giảm ước tính … does not
  have money flow? Even it's an assumption? You can make it grey or opacity.” So the stress loss, a news item's or a
  policy item's impact, an active scenario's effect and a goal's monthly saving each get a grey, dashed pill. It does
  not follow the period, since there is no history behind it. Concentration and the reserve risk are levels, not
  moves, so they have none.
- **On the line or beside it.** A pill sits on its spoke where no money runs along it (ownership spokes have no
  sparks). Where sparks run (money in and out, repayments, estimates), it sits beside the spoke. It tries five places
  along the spoke and is left out if none is clear of the discs, the labels, the other pills and the *other* spokes.
  The last rule was added after the phone shot showed “+177 triệu” and “+740 triệu” stacked beside the same line.
  With it, all nine money spokes of C01's Tổng quan have a pill at 1440×900 and at 390×844.
- **A folded group of classes sums its members** (“+3 tài sản khác” +40 triệu).
- **The phone's graph controls wrap.** The new switch made the row 30 px wider than a 390 px screen.
- **C01S's older income is 60 % of today's.** Her fixture year saves ~70 triệu a month against 350 triệu of cash, so
  going back her cash would go below zero. The generator lowers the older years' income in 5 % steps until cash stays
  above −50 triệu (a few days just under zero show as zero). Every other person keeps 100 %.

**Measured:**
- Every holding of the 7 people: today's value, the month-ago anchor and the 1 January anchor are met to 1.000 đ.
  Three holdings bought after 1 January are 0 that day.
- Running the generator twice gives byte-identical files (7 files, 772 kB).
- C01: month change +440 triệu = Tổng quan's; the classes sum to net worth by day, month and year.
- Net worth rises from 2021 for every family: C01 16,4 → 24,5 tỷ, C02 64,4 → 95,3 tỷ, C05 2,1 → 3,5 tỷ.
- Screens: desktop and phone, light and dark — 0 page errors, no horizontal scroll at 390 or 360 px.
